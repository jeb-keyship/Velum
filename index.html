How it works

A passage plan, not a straight line.

You get a chart and a waypoint table you can work from: course, distance, speed through the water and speed made good on every leg.

Live currents

Several leading ocean-current forecasts blended into one field, so a voyage crosses from one to the next without a seam.

Recommended routes

A recommendation, not an order. Velum plans around charted safety fairways, traffic separation schemes, the Florida Keys PSSA and a five-mile offshore standoff, and gives your officers the data to make the call.

ETA, fuel and CII

Give a speed and get an ETA, or a required arrival and get the speed. Fuel, CO₂ and an IMO CII rating on every route.
