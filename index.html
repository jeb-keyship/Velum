02 — APPROACH

Built from the bridge, not the boardroom.

Most routing tools are built by software teams that have never stood a watch. Velum starts from operational reality — voyage planning, current behavior, and the judgment calls that happen on deck — and layers data science on top of that experience, not the other way around.

→

03 — ABOUT

Founded on deck, not in a lab.

Velum was founded by a licensed deck officer working aboard oil tankers, combining hands-on navigation and voyage-planning experience with a background in data analysis and oceanography. The goal is simple: give mariners routing tools that reflect the way voyages actually work at sea.
