PLATFORM

Built on real charts, real currents, real rules.

01

Live Current Data

Stitches three real NOAA ocean current models (AMSEAS, USEAST, GoMOFS) by priority into one continuous field, so a route from the Gulf to Maine has real data the whole way — not extrapolation past a model's edge.

02

Federal Regulatory Compliance

Routes obey traffic separation schemes, particularly sensitive sea areas, and safety fairways verified section-by-section against 33 CFR Parts 166 and 167 — not inferred from summaries.

03

Minimum-Time Optimization

Runs a time-dependent search over a current-aware mesh to find the fastest track, then compares it honestly against the shortest safe rhumb line — so you see the real benefit in hours, not just nautical miles.

04

Chart-Style Output

Plots the result as an annotated navigational chart: colorized current streamlines, TSS and fairway overlays, numbered waypoints, and a route summary — console output plus a saved chart image.

APPROACH

Built from the bridge, not the boardroom.

Most routing tools are built by software teams that have never stood a watch. Velum starts from operational reality — voyage planning, current behavior, and the judgment calls that happen on deck — and layers data science and AI-assisted analysis on top of that experience, using AI to accelerate research, verification, and modeling while every routing decision still has to hold up against real charts, real regulations, and real seamanship.

ABOUT

Founded on deck, not in a lab.

Velum was founded by a licensed deck officer working aboard oil tankers, who built the routing engine from the ground up — starting with live NOAA current models, real navigational chart data, and the federal regulations that govern where a tanker can and can't go. The goal is simple: give mariners a routing tool that reflects the way voyages actually work at sea, not a generic shortest-path approximation.
